EditSutra

A Skillverse product: an AI-powered Adobe Premiere Pro extension that automates about 80% of repetitive editing work. I spotted the bottlenecks in my own editing work, designed it around how editors really work, and built it myself with Claude Code.

In beta with early users · Mac and Windows · 100+ tools

  • AI-assisted rough cuts
  • Automatic removal of silences, filler words and bad retakes
  • Automated repetitive editing workflows
  • One-click editing recipes
  • AI Shorts finder with hook scoring
  • Automatic 9:16 vertical reframing
  • Long video to Shorts and Reels workflows
  • English, Hindi and Hinglish captions
  • Animated captions and text animations
  • Built-in motion graphics and map animations
  • Ready-to-use creative templates
  • Voice cleanup
  • Sound design
  • Music ducking
  • AI-assisted audio processing
  • B-roll matching
  • Auto zoom and transitions
  • Automatic colour correction and grading tools
  • 27 LUTs and 103 presets
  • 142 templates
  • 97 built-in sounds
  • Ready-to-use editing assets and workflows

Everything stays inside Premiere Pro, so editors don't switch between 6–8 websites. Launch plan: lifetime licence, free trial and referrals.
